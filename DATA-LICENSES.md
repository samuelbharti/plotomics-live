# Licenses of the bundled data

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the code in this repository. It does **not**
cover the third-party datasets vendored under `data/` and `www/data/`, which
keep the licenses of their own sources.

Those licenses include:

- **CC BY 4.0** — 10x Genomics Visium and Xenium, UniProtKB, AlphaFold DB, and
  Tahoe-100M.
- **CC0** — InterPro.
- **ODbL** — cBioPortal, over open-access TCGA data.

If you redistribute this repository, those terms travel with the data.

`data/prep/PROVENANCE.md` is the authoritative list: it names the source, the
license, and the retrieval date for every dataset. Read it before you add a new
one.
